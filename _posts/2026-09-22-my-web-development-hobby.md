  <h2>Back to 1999 🦖</h2>
  <p>This story goes all the way back to 1999. Yes, the age of “What is the internet?” If you were a teenager or young adult then, you might have had the same thought I did: let’s build a website!</p>
  <p>I was hooked. My first website was built entirely with HTML, so you can probably imagine what it looked like. Before long, the fad passed, and so did my website.</p>
  <p>But my interest in web development never really disappeared. It just had to share space with all my other interests.</p>

  <h2>A hobby I kept coming back to</h2>
  <p>In college, I took an elective web development class. By then, everything had changed. There was Dreamweaver and other visual tools to explore. It wasn’t my major, so I was mostly having fun, but I could see how big the skill gap was between professionals and an amateur like me.</p>

  <h2>You never know which function will get called</h2>
  <p>The funny thing is, life is like a computer program: you never know which function will get called. 😂</p>
  <p>When I was looking for a job in Türkiye, I came across a web content management company that needed someone with Chinese language skills and basic website-building experience. I got hired!</p>
  <p>I spent about two years managing web content for Air France and KLM through that company. I learned so much more there, and a hobby I had picked up out of curiosity became part of my working life.</p>

  <h2>Building for the community</h2>
  <p>After I moved to Orlando, I wasn’t able to continue in the same job. Around that time, the Orlando Chinese Association (OCA) had just been founded and was looking for someone to build its website. I volunteered.</p>
  <p>I built the website, then eventually stepped away from the team because I had other priorities. In early 2026, I decided to return as a volunteer to help manage the website again.</p>
  <p>Looking back, I found plenty of funny—and questionable—design choices I had made about ten years earlier! With AI’s help, I can build much more these days, and it has been fun to explore what’s possible.</p>
  <p><a class="button" href="http://www.orlcna.org">Visit the OCA website ↗</a></p>

  <h2>Still curious, still building</h2>
  <p>I really enjoy solving problems by building websites, features, and applications. It keeps me learning and up to date, and it makes me feel young and open-minded.</p>
  <p>I like fresh ideas and new perspectives. If someone has a suggestion or a challenge for me, I’m happy to take it on. I like helping people, too, so if you need help with a website, I’m right here!</p>
